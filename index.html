Annie Lee Talks Tech

Annie Lee Talks Tech is a daily one-minute tech news show. Every morning, Annie explains the day's biggest tech story — what happened, how it works, and why it matters — in a short video published to the channel's own YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, and X accounts.

This site is the official website of the Annie Lee Talks Tech channel and of the channel's private publishing app, a single-creator tool that runs on the creator's own computer and publishes her own videos to her own accounts.
