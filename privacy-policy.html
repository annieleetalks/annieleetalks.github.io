Privacy Policy — Annie Lee Talks Tech

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Annie Lee Talks Tech ("we", "the channel") operates a small, single-creator automation tool (the "App") that publishes the channel's own daily videos to the channel's own accounts on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, and X. This policy explains what information the App accesses and how it is used.

Who the App serves

The App is used by exactly one person: the owner of the Annie Lee Talks Tech channel. It is not offered to the public, has no sign-up, and no other person can authorize it or be authorized by it.

Information the App accesses

  • Platform authorization tokens. When the channel owner authorizes the App on a platform (for example TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, or X), the platform issues an access/refresh token. The token is stored only on the owner's own computer, in a file readable only by the owner's account.
  • The channel's own content. The App reads and uploads video files created by the channel owner, together with their titles, descriptions, and captions, solely to publish them to the owner's own accounts.
  • The channel's own account basics. The App may read the owner's own account identifier and channel name to confirm it is posting to the correct account.

What the App does NOT do

  • It does not collect, access, or store information about any other person or account — no followers, viewers, commenters, or third parties.
  • It does not sell, rent, share, or disclose any information to anyone.
  • It does not use advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.
  • It does not post, message, or act on any account other than the channel owner's own accounts.

Data retention and deletion

Authorization tokens remain on the owner's computer until the owner revokes the App's access on the platform or deletes the stored token, either of which stops the App from acting immediately. No data is kept on any external server operated by us.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact information listed on the Annie Lee Talks Tech YouTube channel.
